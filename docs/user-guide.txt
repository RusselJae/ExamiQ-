EXAMIQ+ USER GUIDE
==================

This guide explains how to use the ExamiQ+ Self-Regulated Mathematics Exam
Review System. It covers what each role does, how to navigate the system,
and how data flows from setup through review sessions to analytics.


1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW
------------------

ExamiQ+ is a web-based mathematics exam review platform for college students.
It supports self-regulated learning through timed review sessions, confidence
calibration tracking, mistake logging, adaptive question selection, and
role-based analytics for faculty and department leadership.

The system is organized around degree programs. Each program (for example,
Computer Science, Information Technology, BSEd Mathematics, Psychology,
Marketing, Human Resources, Hospitality Management, and Criminology) has
its own math subjects, topics, and question bank. Students review content
matched to their home degree program and section.

There are four user roles:

  - Student
  - Faculty (displayed in the interface; stored in the system as professor)
  - Department Chairperson
  - Campus Administrator

How the roles connect:

  1. The Campus Administrator sets up academic years, terms, and program
     sections (cohorts with capacity limits).
  2. The Department Chairperson assigns faculty to sections, subjects, and
     terms. This creates course offerings and exam setup records.
  3. Faculty configure exam settings, build the question bank, and monitor
     student performance.
  4. Students complete timed review sessions. Answers, confidence levels,
     and mistakes are recorded.
  5. Analytics and reports feed back to students (self-regulation), faculty
     (interventions), and chairpersons (program oversight).


2. GETTING STARTED (ALL USERS)
------------------------------

2.1 Accessing the system

  - Open the ExamiQ+ website in a web browser.
  - Log in at /accounts/login/
  - Create a new account at /accounts/signup/
  - Log out at /accounts/logout/

2.2 After login

Each role is redirected to its home page:

  - Student          -> /student/dashboard/
  - Faculty          -> /professor/courses/
  - Chairperson      -> /chairperson/dashboard/
  - Campus Admin     -> /admin/ (Django admin) or /campus/ via sidebar

2.3 Profile and settings

All authenticated users can manage their account at /profile/:

  - Update name and profile photo
  - Request an email change (verification required)
  - Change password

Students can also update program, year level, and section on their profile.
These fields are required before a student can start a review session.

Notifications are available at /profile/notifications/. Users can view,
mark as read, and check unread counts.

2.4 Registration and approval

Public signup is available for three roles:

  Student
    - Signs up with student number (9 digits), program, year level, section,
      and phone number.
    - Account is approved and activated immediately.
    - Can log in and use the system right away.

  Faculty
    - Signs up with employee ID.
    - Account starts as pending and inactive.
    - Must wait for a Campus Administrator to approve before logging in.

  Department Chairperson
    - Signs up with employee ID and department.
    - Account starts as pending and inactive.
    - Must wait for a Campus Administrator to approve before logging in.

  Campus Administrator
    - Not available on the public signup page.
    - Accounts are created by existing administrators or system seeding.

When a pending or rejected user tries to log in, they are redirected to the
login page with a message indicating their registration status.

Campus Administrators receive email and in-app notifications when new
faculty or chairperson registrations are submitted.


3. ROLE: STUDENT
----------------

3.1 Purpose

Students use ExamiQ+ to practice mathematics through timed exam-style review
sessions, track their mistakes and weak areas, and build awareness of how
well their confidence matches their actual performance (calibration).

3.2 Navigation (sidebar)

Main:
  - Dashboard        (/student/dashboard/)
  - Start Review     (/student/review/setup/)

Insights:
  - Session History  (/student/sessions/)
  - Mistakes         (/student/mistakes/)
  - Weak Areas       (/student/mistakes/patterns/)
  - Topic Progress   (/student/topics/)

All users also have Settings (profile) and Log out in the sidebar footer.

3.3 Prerequisites before starting a review

Before the Start Review page allows a session, the system checks:

  - The student profile has a section and year level set.
  - The student's year level matches their assigned section.
  - A current academic term is active (set by Campus Administrator).
  - At least one teaching assignment exists for the student's section with
    exam setup enabled by faculty.

If any requirement is missing, the setup page shows an eligibility message
explaining what needs to be completed.

3.4 Starting a review session (step by step)

  1. Go to Start Review (/student/review/setup/).
  2. Select a subject from those available for the student's program and
     section.
  3. Select a topic within that subject.
  4. Select a difficulty level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
     Available difficulties may be limited by faculty exam setup.
  5. Confirm or adjust the session duration in minutes.
  6. Submit the setup form to begin the session.

Optional pre-session prompts (confidence level and session goal) may appear
before the exam begins.

3.5 During the timed exam

  - The session runs on a countdown timer with a per-question time limit.
  - Questions are selected adaptively. The system prioritizes topics where
    the student has prior mistakes, questions answered wrong with high
    inferred confidence, and questions not yet seen.
  - The student selects an answer for each question before time runs out.
  - Confidence is inferred from response time, not from a manual rating
    after each answer.
  - Explanations are not shown during the exam. They appear on the session
    summary after the exam ends.
  - The session continues until all questions are answered or time expires.

3.6 After the session

  - The student is taken to the session summary (/student/review/<id>/summary/).
  - The summary shows accuracy, calibration insights (mastery, misconception,
    lucky guess, expected gap), and review recommendations for future study.
  - Completed sessions appear in Session History.

3.7 Using analytics and self-regulation tools

  Dashboard
    Performance overview, calibration summary, and recommended topics or
    actions based on recent sessions.

  Session History
    List of past completed and expired review sessions.

  Mistakes
    Browse individual wrong answers. Open an answer detail page to review
    the question, the student's response, and step-by-step explanations.
    Optional AI-generated feedback can be requested on mistake detail pages
    when AI is enabled on the server.

  Weak Areas
    View mistake patterns grouped by topic. Drill into a topic to review
    all answers in that area.

  Topic Progress
    Track mastery progress across math topics in the student's program.


4. ROLE: FACULTY (PROFESSOR)
----------------------------

4.1 Purpose

Faculty manage course offerings, configure exam access for students, build
and submit questions for approval, monitor class performance, identify
students who need intervention, and improve explanations on commonly
missed questions.

4.2 Navigation (sidebar)

Main:
  - Overview         (/professor/overview/)
  - My Courses       (/professor/courses/)

Per course (when a course is selected):
  - Analytics        (/professor/courses/<id>/)
  - Roster           (/professor/courses/<id>/roster/)
  - Exam Setup       (/professor/courses/<id>/exam-setup/)
  - Heatmap          (/professor/courses/<id>/heatmap/)
  - Topics           (/professor/courses/<id>/topics/)
  - Question Bank    (/professor/courses/<id>/questions/)
  - Feedback         (/professor/courses/<id>/feedback/)

4.3 Course access

Faculty see courses in two ways:

  Chairperson-assigned courses
    When the department chairperson has created teaching assignments, the
    faculty member's courses are created automatically from those
    assignments. Self-creating new courses is blocked in this case.
    Contact the chairperson to change teaching load.

  Self-created courses
    When no chairperson assignments exist, faculty can create their own
    course offerings at /professor/courses/create/. They set term, year,
    section, and program. Courses can be cloned to a new term or archived
    when no longer needed.

Faculty can only access and manage courses they own.

4.4 Exam setup

At /professor/courses/<id>/exam-setup/, faculty configure:

  - Whether exams are enabled for students in the assigned section
  - Session duration in minutes
  - Seconds allowed per question
  - Which topics students may select
  - Which difficulty levels are available

Exam setup must be enabled for students in that section to see the subject
on their Start Review page.

4.5 Review windows (optional)

Faculty can create scheduled review windows at
/professor/courses/<id>/windows/. Windows define named review periods with
start and end dates. Note: the primary student review path uses Exam Setup
settings rather than window-based session launch.

4.6 Topics

Faculty manage course topics at /professor/courses/<id>/topics/. They can
create, edit, and delete topics tied to subjects within the course program.

4.7 Question bank

Faculty build questions at /professor/courses/<id>/questions/:

  Single question create
    Add one question with stem, choices, correct answer, explanation steps,
    topic, and difficulty.

  Batch create
    Build multiple questions in one session using a draft queue.

  Edit and deactivate
  - Edit existing questions. Approved questions return to pending status
    when edited and must be re-approved by the chairperson.
  - Deactivate questions no longer needed.

  AI-assisted tools (when AI is enabled on the server)
  - Generate draft questions from a topic and difficulty
  - Validate draft questions for structure and quality
  - Suggest difficulty for a question
  - Generate variations of an existing question

All faculty-created questions start with status PENDING. They are not used
in student sessions until a chairperson approves them.

4.8 Feedback

At /professor/courses/<id>/feedback/, faculty can edit step-by-step
explanations for approved questions that students frequently miss. This
helps clarify common errors after review sessions.

4.9 Analytics and interventions

  Overview
    Cross-course summary cards for all of the faculty member's courses.

  Course analytics
    Per-course performance summary, calibration breakdown, and intervention
    list flagging students who may need support (for example, low accuracy,
    calibration gaps, or insufficient practice).

  Roster
    Student list with performance bands. Drill into an individual student for
    course-specific accuracy, calibration analysis, and session history.

  Heatmap
    Topic mastery heatmap showing class-wide strengths and weaknesses.

  Intervention CSV export
    Download a CSV from /professor/courses/<id>/interventions/export/
    containing student names, intervention flags, accuracy, and suggested
    actions.

  AI course summary
    Generate a narrative course report from the analytics page when AI is
    enabled.


5. ROLE: DEPARTMENT CHAIRPERSON
-------------------------------

5.1 Purpose

The chairperson oversees mathematics review activity within their department.
They assign faculty teaching loads, approve question bank submissions,
and monitor program-level performance across all degree programs managed
by the department.

5.2 Scope

A chairperson's access is limited to their own department. They can only
manage assignments, approve questions, and view analytics for programs
where their department is the managing department.

5.3 Navigation (sidebar)

  - Programs           (/chairperson/dashboard/)
  - Assignments        (/chairperson/assignments/)
  - Question Review    (/chairperson/questions/review/)
  - Cross-Program      (/chairperson/analytics/by-program/)
  - Course Audit       (/chairperson/courses/)

The Question Review menu shows a badge when pending questions are waiting
for approval.

5.4 Teaching assignments

At /chairperson/assignments/, the chairperson links:

  - A faculty member
  - A program section (cohort)
  - A subject
  - An academic term

Creating an assignment automatically creates or links a course offering and
an exam setup record for that faculty member. This is the primary way
students gain access to review subjects for their section.

Assignments can be deleted when they are no longer needed.

5.5 Question review

At /chairperson/questions/review/, the chairperson sees all PENDING
questions submitted by faculty for programs in their department.

For each question, the chairperson can:

  Approve
    Question status becomes APPROVED and it becomes available in student
    review sessions.

  Reject
    Question status becomes REJECTED. An optional note can be left for the
    faculty member.

  Edit and approve
    Open the question, make corrections, save, and approve in one step.

5.6 Oversight analytics

  Programs dashboard
    Per-program performance summaries for all programs in the department.

  Cross-Program analytics
    Department-wide math analytics broken down by home degree program.

  Course audit
    List of course offerings that have review activity from students in
    department programs.


6. ROLE: CAMPUS ADMINISTRATOR
-----------------------------

6.1 Purpose

The Campus Administrator maintains the institutional infrastructure that
all other roles depend on: user accounts, program sections, and the academic
calendar. They approve or reject faculty and chairperson registration
requests and can provision accounts directly.

6.2 Navigation (sidebar)

  - Campus Dashboard    (/campus/)
  - Users               (/campus/users/)
  - Sections            (/campus/sections/)
  - Academic Calendar   (/campus/academic-calendar/)

Campus Administrators and system superusers see this section in addition
to any other role-specific navigation they may have.

6.3 User management

At /campus/users/, the administrator can:

  - List and filter all users by role and approval status
  - Approve pending faculty and chairperson registrations
  - Reject pending registrations
  - Activate or deactivate existing accounts (cannot deactivate own account)
  - Provision new faculty accounts at /campus/users/create/professor/
  - Provision new chairperson accounts at /campus/users/create/chairperson/

Approved users receive an email notification and can log in immediately.
Provisioned accounts are created in an approved, active state.

6.4 Section management

At /campus/sections/, the administrator manages program sections (cohorts):

  - Create individual sections with program, year level, academic year, and
    capacity
  - Bulk-create sections
  - Edit section details and capacity

Sections are used during student signup and chairperson teaching assignments.
Capacity limits are enforced when students register.

6.5 Academic calendar

At /campus/academic-calendar/, the administrator:

  - Creates academic year records (for example, 2025-2026)
  - Creates terms within each year (for example, 1st Semester, 2nd Semester)
  - Sets which year and term are currently active

Students cannot start review sessions unless a current academic term is set.


7. END-TO-END SYSTEM FLOW
---------------------------

7.1 Flow A: Account lifecycle

  1. User visits the ExamiQ+ landing page.
  2. User signs up at /accounts/signup/ and selects a role.
  3. Student accounts are activated immediately.
     Faculty and chairperson accounts enter pending status.
  4. Campus Administrator reviews pending registrations at /campus/users/.
  5. Administrator approves or rejects the account.
  6. Approved user logs in and is redirected to their role dashboard.
  7. User maintains profile and notification settings at /profile/.

7.2 Flow B: Content and access pipeline

  1. Campus Administrator creates academic years, terms, and program sections.
  2. Campus Administrator sets the current academic year and term.
  3. Chairperson creates teaching assignments linking faculty, section,
     subject, and term.
  4. System creates course offering and exam setup for each assignment.
  5. Faculty enables exam setup and configures topics, difficulties, and timing.
  6. Faculty creates questions in the question bank (manually or with AI).
  7. All new questions enter PENDING status.
  8. Chairperson reviews and approves questions.
  9. Approved questions become available for student review sessions.

7.3 Flow C: Student review cycle

  1. Student completes profile (program, year level, section).
  2. Student opens Start Review and passes eligibility checks.
  3. Student selects subject, topic, difficulty, and duration.
  4. System creates a timed review session.
  5. Adaptive engine selects questions from approved, active bank items.
  6. Student answers each question within the time limit.
  7. System grades each answer, infers confidence from response time, and
     logs mistakes for wrong answers.
  8. Session ends when questions are exhausted or time expires.
  9. Student views session summary with calibration insights and
     recommendations.
  10. Data feeds into Dashboard, Session History, Mistakes, Weak Areas,
      and Topic Progress.

7.4 Flow D: Instructional response

  1. Faculty opens course analytics or roster.
  2. System flags students on the intervention list based on accuracy,
     calibration gaps, or practice frequency.
  3. Faculty drills into individual student performance.
  4. Faculty exports intervention CSV for follow-up or advising.
  5. Faculty edits explanations on high-mistake questions in Feedback.
  6. Faculty may generate an AI course summary for reporting.

7.5 Flow E: Institutional oversight

  1. Chairperson views Programs dashboard for department-wide performance.
  2. Chairperson monitors pending questions and approves or rejects submissions.
  3. Chairperson reviews Cross-Program analytics across degree programs.
  4. Chairperson audits which courses have active student review participation.


8. QUESTION LIFECYCLE (REFERENCE)
---------------------------------

  Faculty creates question
       |
       v
    PENDING  ---- Chairperson rejects ---->  REJECTED
       |
       v
  Chairperson approves
       |
       v
    APPROVED (active in student sessions)

If faculty edits an approved question, it returns to PENDING and must be
re-approved before it is used in sessions again.

Faculty can deactivate questions at any time. Deactivated questions are
removed from the active pool regardless of status.


9. QUICK REFERENCE: ROLE PERMISSIONS
------------------------------------

Student
  Can do:
    - Start timed review sessions for their program and section
    - View personal dashboard, session history, mistakes, and topic progress
    - Request AI feedback on individual wrong answers (when AI enabled)
    - Update profile including program, year level, and section
  Cannot do:
    - Access faculty, chairperson, or campus admin pages
    - Create or approve questions
    - View other students' data

Faculty
  Can do:
    - Manage own course offerings (create, clone, archive when allowed)
    - Configure exam setup and review windows
    - Create, edit, and deactivate questions (submitted as PENDING)
    - Use AI tools for question generation and validation (when AI enabled)
    - View course analytics, roster, heatmap, and intervention exports
    - Edit feedback on high-mistake approved questions
  Cannot do:
    - Approve own questions
    - Access other faculty members' courses
    - Manage campus-wide users, sections, or calendar
    - Self-create courses when chairperson assignments exist

Department Chairperson
  Can do:
    - Create and delete teaching assignments in own department
    - Approve, reject, or edit-then-approve pending questions
    - View program dashboard, cross-program analytics, and course audit
  Cannot do:
    - Access other departments' assignments or analytics
    - Approve questions for programs outside managing department
    - Manage campus infrastructure (sections, calendar)

Campus Administrator
  Can do:
    - Approve or reject faculty and chairperson registrations
    - Provision faculty and chairperson accounts directly
    - Activate or deactivate user accounts
    - Manage program sections and academic calendar
    - View campus dashboard with user counts and activity feed
  Cannot do:
    - Sign up through the public registration page
    - Approve student accounts (students are auto-approved)


END OF GUIDE
