About ExamiQ+

ExamiQ+ helps BSEd Mathematics students prepare with timed exams, explicit confidence ratings, mistake history, and post-session feedback. Faculty set up exams, manage the question bank, and use analytics and chat to support the sections and subjects they handle.

What we offer

System Features

Feedback & Explanations

Practice shows step-by-step feedback after each answer; timed exams unlock explanations when you finish.

Mistake Tracking

Incorrect answers logged by topic, date, and confidence level for structured review.

Performance Analytics

Dashboards and heatmaps for accuracy, confidence patterns, and course-level insights.

AI Tutor

Ask for guided help on mistakes without giving away the full answer up front.

Faculty–Student Chat

Students raise concerns on mistakes; faculty reply for the sections and subjects they handle.

Question Bank & Exam Setup

Faculty author questions (including AI-assisted drafts) and control which courses students can take.
